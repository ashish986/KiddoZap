Frequently Asked Questions

Is KiddoZap safe for children?

KiddoZap has no accounts, chat or in-game purchases, and it does not ask for names, addresses, phone numbers or locations. The games are non-violent, but we suggest that parents supervise younger children. The site shows ads, so please read our Privacy Policy.

Are the games free, and do I need to download anything?

All games are free and run directly in the browser on phones, tablets and computers. There is nothing to install and no sign-up.

Can puzzle and math games help with schoolwork?

They can be good practice. Sudoku encourages step-by-step reasoning, Math Sprint gives quick repeated practice with number facts and Memory Match exercises visual memory. Games are a fun extra, not a replacement for lessons and real practice.

Can teachers use KiddoZap in class?

Many teachers like short games for warm-ups and breaks. Every school network is different, so please check your school rules and ask your IT team if a game does not open. Our classroom guide has ideas.
