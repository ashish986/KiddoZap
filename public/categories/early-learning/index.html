Early Childhood & Pre-K Learning Games

Nursery Rhymes, Magic Piano, Colors & Shape Matching

Pre-K & Early Years

Tailored for our youngest learners (Ages 2 to 7). These colorful, gentle mini-games build phonological awareness, pitch harmony, color discrimination, and foundational hand-eye coordination.

Gentle games for the youngest players

For children aged about 2 to 7, the best games are slow, colourful and easy to understand. Nursery rhymes build listening and vocabulary, memory games practise matching, and tapping games build early hand-eye coordination.

At this age, play together whenever you can: name the colours and animals, sing along and keep sessions short. Ten minutes is plenty, and the screen should go off well before bedtime. Our screen time guide has simple habits to follow.
