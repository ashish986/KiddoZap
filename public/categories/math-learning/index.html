Mental Math & Arithmetic Fluency Games

Rapid Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication & Number Sense

Math & STEM

Transform repetitive math homework into an exhilarating speed challenge! Practice addition, subtraction, multiplication times tables, and division in low-stress gamified environments.

Practice that feels like play

Number facts such as 7 plus 8 or 6 times 7 become easier when children see them often in short bursts. A game can make that repetition feel lighter, because a wrong answer just means another try.

Short rounds of about five minutes work best. When your child gets stuck on a fact, write it down and come back to it the next day. For quiz-style and printable practice, see our free times table practice and multiplication chart.

Free practice tools

  • Times Table Practice: quiz yourself on any table from 2 to 12.
  • Math Facts Practice: addition, subtraction and division in short rounds.
  • Multiplication Chart: a printable 1 to 12 chart.
