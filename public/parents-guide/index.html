A grown-up’s guide to KiddoZap

Pick a short activity, play a round together, and follow it with something you can do away from the screen.

Start with something familiar

Ask your child to name the picture in ABC Garden. Say the first sound slowly together. If the choice is tricky, point to two letters and say each one aloud.

In Counting Basket, help them touch one fruit for each number they say. Ask “How many altogether?” after you reach the last fruit.

Notice how they figured it out

In Shape Picnic, notice the long edges and corners. In Pattern Picnic, clap once for each picture and say the repeating part together. Give your child time to make a second choice.

Choose a stopping point together

Play one round or listen to one rhyme, then use the off-screen activities to keep the idea going. Let your child point to what they want to try next time.
