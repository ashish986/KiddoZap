Parents & Educators Guide

KiddoZap was founded to provide a clean, secure, and educational online space for children and teens.

Zero Personal Data Collected

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords. Children can play immediately without registering an account.

Safe Content Guarantee

All 24+ games feature non-violent, colorful themes. There are no multiplayer chat rooms or open message boards, keeping children safe from online strangers.

Balanced Screen Time

We suggest limiting gaming to 20-30 minute focused intervals followed by outdoor play and homework.
