KiddoZap Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 24, 2026 • Lead Engineer & Founder: Ashish Pal (ashishpal996@gmail.com)

Welcome to KiddoZap (https://kiddozap.com). Protecting the privacy and digital well-being of young students, children, and families is the foundational requirement of our platform. This document describes how our website operates, our zero-harvesting architecture, and our third-party advertising disclosures.

1. Identity of Data Controller and Platform Operator

KiddoZap is an independent web application authored, maintained, and operated by software engineer Ashish Pal, located in New Delhi, India. Inquiries regarding this policy may be dispatched directly to support@kiddozap.com or ashishpal996@gmail.com.

2. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) Compliance

KiddoZap operates in full conformance with the United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA, 15 U.S.C. 6501–6505) and the Federal Trade Commission's COPPA Rule (16 CFR Part 312):

  • Zero Mandatory Accounts: Children, students, and visitors are never required or requested to create accounts, provide passwords, or disclose their identities. All 20 games load immediately upon clicking.
  • Zero Collection of Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect names, street addresses, postal codes, email addresses, phone numbers, audio recordings, or biometric data from any child or adult.
  • No Unmoderated Communication Channels: KiddoZap does not provide chat boxes, private messaging, public leaderboards with user-submitted text, or social discussion threads. Children can never be messaged, solicited, or contacted by digital strangers while playing on KiddoZap.

3. Google AdSense, DoubleClick DART Cookies & Third-Party Advertising

To support server hosting, bandwidth, and ongoing game development while keeping access 100% free for schools and families, KiddoZap displays online advertisements served by Google AdSense:

  • Use of Cookies: Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve advertisements on KiddoZap.
  • DoubleClick DART Cookie: Google's use of the DART cookie enables it to serve advertisements to our users based on their prior visits to our website and other websites across the Internet.
  • Family-Safe & Sensitive Category Restrictions: We enforce strict content filters in our AdSense account to prohibit advertisements related to adult content, violence, gambling, dating, financial speculation, or age-inappropriate media.
  • Opt-Out Mechanism: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Advertising and Privacy Policy or managing preferences via Google Ads Settings.

4. Client-Side Browser Storage (LocalStorage)

KiddoZap utilizes standard modern browser LocalStorage strictly on your local device to enhance the player experience:

  • Personal High Scores: High scores in games like Math Sprint Blitz, Snake Neon, and Memory Match are saved locally in your own browser cache.
  • Sound Toggles: Your audio mute or volume preference is remembered locally between visits.
  • Zero Server Transmission: This data never leaves your computer or smartphone. We do not transmit, analyze, or sync your game scores to any remote server or third party. You can clear your scores at any time by clearing your browser cache.

5. Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) Standards

When used in school computer labs, classrooms, and libraries, KiddoZap functions as an anonymous instructional aid. Because we do not store student education records or personally identifiable student submissions, teachers and educational administrators can integrate KiddoZap games into classroom curricula without risking FERPA compliance issues.

6. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) Rights

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents have specific statutory rights:

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
  • We do not share personal information with third-party data brokers.
  • Because KiddoZap does not compile user profiles, there is no personal dossier maintained for any individual.
