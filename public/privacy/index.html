KiddoZap Privacy Policy & COPPA Disclosure

Last Updated: September 2026 • Strict Student & Child Safety

Welcome to KiddoZap (accessible at https://kiddozap.com/). The privacy and safety of children, students, parents, and educators is our utmost priority. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by KiddoZap and how we utilize and protect it.

Summary of KiddoZap Child Protection Guarantee:
  • We NEVER require user registration, passwords, phone numbers, or real identities.
  • We DO NOT collect or store Personally Identifiable Information (PII) from children under the age of 13.
  • All gameplay scores, star awards, unlocked badges, and preferred sound settings are stored strictly on your local browser device via localStorage.

1. Compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

KiddoZap adheres strictly to the United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy standards:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.
  • No user accounts, profiles, chat rooms, or direct messaging features exist that could allow minors to publicly post or disclose personal details.
  • If a parent or guardian believes that their child has provided personal information to our website, please contact us immediately at support@kiddozap.com, and we will promptly remove such records.

2. Third-Party Advertising & Google AdSense Policies

KiddoZap partners with third-party advertising vendors, including Google AdSense, to support the ongoing development of free educational games. To comply fully with Google's policies and ensure total transparency:

  • Google AdSense & Cookies: Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on KiddoZap. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to KiddoZap and other sites on the Internet.
  • DoubleClick DART Cookie: The DoubleClick cookie is used by Google in ads served on publisher websites. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Third-Party Vendor Disclosures: Third-party ad networks or ad servers use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. KiddoZap has no access to or control over these cookies used by third-party advertisers.
  • Opting Out: You can opt out of personalized advertising from participating ad networks by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices or YourAdChoices.

3. Local Device Storage (No Cloud Profiling)

To enable offline play and preserve child privacy, KiddoZap utilizes client-side Web Storage (HTML5 localStorage) to save:

  • Game high scores and completed puzzle milestones.
  • Earned star rewards and trophy achievements.
  • Audio mute preferences and user interface theme settings.

This data remains stored exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or third-party analytical databases.

4. Log Files & Technical Diagnostics

Like standard web servers, our hosting infrastructure automatically logs basic technical information when requests are made to https://kiddozap.com/. This information includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable and are used solely for analyzing traffic trends, administering the site, and preventing DDoS attacks.

5. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / GDPR-K) Rights

Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom enjoy specific statutory data rights:

  • The right to access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • The right to erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your data under certain conditions.

Since KiddoZap operates without requiring personal registrations, clearing your browser cache and local storage instantly erases all local gameplay telemetry.
