Firefly Meadow

Getting your game ready…

For grown-ups

One firefly appears at a time and stays until you find it. Look across the meadow, then tap its spot. Each new firefly lights another lantern.

Try it together, away from the screen

Try a gentle follow-the-light activity indoors. Point to one picture in a book, wait for your child to find it, then let them choose a picture for you to spot. Take turns following a point from left to right and top to bottom.

You can take a break between fireflies. The meadow has no timer.
