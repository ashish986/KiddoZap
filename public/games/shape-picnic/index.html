Shape Picnic

Getting your game ready…

For grown-ups

Compare the silhouette on the mat with three solid pieces. Children can use a corner or a curved edge as a clue. Color changes between pieces, so the outline tells you what fits.

Try it together, away from the screen

Trace around a cup and a small box on paper. Set the cup and box beside the outlines and let your child match them. Turn the box and ask them to compare the corners again. Try finding a circle, square or rectangle elsewhere in the room.

To tell a square from a rectangle in this game, compare the sides. The square has four equal sides and the rectangle shown here has two longer sides.
