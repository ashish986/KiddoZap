Pattern Picnic

Getting your game ready…

For grown-ups

Listen to the same small group of pictures repeat. Some rounds alternate two pictures, while others repeat a group of three. Saying the pictures out loud can make the repeating part easier to notice.

Try it together, away from the screen

Line up two kinds of safe household objects, such as sock, spoon, sock, spoon. Ask your child to add the next item. Switch roles and let them make a pattern for you to finish. If your child is ready, try sock, sock, spoon as the part that repeats.

When you see apple, leaf, apple, leaf, the repeating group is apple–leaf. Start that group again with an apple.
