Memory game

Picture Pairs

Getting your game ready…

For grown-ups

Each board hides four pairs of animals. Say each animal name when you turn a card over. Remember its place with clues such as “top corner” or “beside the cat.”

Try it together, away from the screen

Draw two simple stars, two circles and two hearts on separate scraps of paper. Mix them, then lay them face down. Start with two pairs if four feels like too much. Let your child pick one card for you and describe where it was.

If two cards differ, they turn back over. You can use what you saw to make the next choice.
