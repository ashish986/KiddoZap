📖 Game Overview & Lore

Speed Tunnel Rush 3D launches players down a kaleidoscopic neon cylindrical conduit at breakneck speeds. The tunnel features flashing polygonal facets and rotating triangular wedges, semi-circular hazard walls, and shifting color barriers. Surviving this supersonic flight requires instantaneous peripheral vision assessment and synchronized circular rotation.

🎮 Complete Game Controls Guide

⌨️ Desktop & Chromebook

Press [LEFT] / [RIGHT] Arrow keys or [A] / [D] to rotate around the circular perimeter of the tunnel.

📱 Touchscreen & Tablet

Tap or hold the left/right sides of the touch display to spin around the tunnel wall.

🏆 Master Strategies & Pro Tips

  • Watch the tunnel center: oncoming obstacles cast faint shadows down the cylinder walls before fully appearing.
  • Rotate continuously towards the widest opening rather than waiting until the last millisecond.
  • Anticipate rotating obstacles: check whether the hazard is spinning clockwise or counterclockwise before passing.
  • Keep your gaze relaxed and rely on peripheral vision to identify clear passage wedges.
  • Take regular breathers between runs to recalibrate your optical reflex system.
