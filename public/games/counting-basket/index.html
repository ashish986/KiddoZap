Counting Basket

Getting your game ready…

For grown-ups

Each round has one to ten pieces of fruit. A check appears when you tap a piece, so it is easier to keep track. The last number said tells you how many pieces are in the whole group.

Try it together, away from the screen

Put three spoons on a table and move each spoon to a second pile as you count. Ask “How many altogether?” Then spread the same spoons farther apart and count again. The answer stays three because the group has the same objects.

For a child just getting started, cover a larger group with your hand and count only the items you uncover together.
