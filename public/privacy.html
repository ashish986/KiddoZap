Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 24, 2026

KiddoZap is committed to protecting the privacy of children and families. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use our website, mobile applications, and educational games.

1. Information We Collect

KiddoZap provides educational and entertainment experiences for children. We do not require children to provide their names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personal information to play basic games.

Depending on the features and services enabled, limited technical information may be processed, including device information, diagnostic reports, crash data, advertising identifiers, and usage information.

We collect or process information only as necessary for legitimate service purposes and as permitted by applicable law.

2. Children's Privacy

KiddoZap is designed with children and families in mind. We aim to comply with applicable children's privacy laws and Google Play's Families Policy.

We do not knowingly require children to create accounts or submit personal information to access basic learning games. If we learn that personal information has been collected inappropriately from a child, we will take reasonable steps to investigate and address the issue, including deletion where appropriate.

Parents and guardians may contact us with questions or requests concerning children's privacy.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

Some versions of KiddoZap may display advertisements to support free games and educational content.

If Google AdMob or another advertising provider is used, that provider may process certain device or usage information to deliver, measure, and maintain advertisements, subject to its privacy policy and applicable law.

For apps intended for children, we will configure advertising services and use eligible advertising SDKs in accordance with applicable Google Play Families requirements. Where required, appropriate child-directed treatment, restrictions on personalized advertising, and other safeguards will be applied.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Cookies and Local Storage

Our website may use cookies, browser storage, or similar technologies to remember preferences, maintain functionality, and understand website performance.

Some games may store scores, progress, or settings locally on your device or in your browser. The Android app may store information differently depending on its implementation.

5. Third-Party Services

KiddoZap may use third-party services for hosting, advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or other app functionality. These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services and according to their own privacy policies.

We do not control the independent privacy practices of third-party providers. We encourage parents and guardians to review the relevant providers' privacy policies.

6. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through KiddoZap. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal, operational, and security requirements. Third-party providers may have their own retention practices.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell children's personal information. Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate, secure, or improve KiddoZap, provide advertisements, comply with legal obligations, or protect our users and services.

Any such processing will be subject to applicable law and safeguards required for children's services.

8. Parents' and Guardians' Rights

Parents and guardians may contact us with questions about information that may have been collected through KiddoZap. Where applicable law provides rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, we will review and respond to requests in accordance with that law.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, or legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage parents and guardians to review this page periodically.
