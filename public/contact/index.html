Contact KiddoZap

For help with the website or Android app, email the KiddoZap support team.

Support email

Email support@kiddozap.com

Reporting a website issue

Include the game or page name, the device and browser you used, and what happened. For piano or rhyme audio, say whether other audio on your device plays normally.

Reporting an Android app issue

Include the app version, Android version and the activity you were using. Leave out a child’s name and other personal details.
