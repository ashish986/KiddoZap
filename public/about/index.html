About KiddoZap

KiddoZap is an Android learning app with a browser play corner for young children and their grown-ups.

What families can try here

Start with familiar pictures in ABC Garden, group fruit in Counting Basket, or compare shapes and patterns. Picture Pairs and Firefly Meadow offer a short play break. Each browser game has a simple activity for grown-ups to try with children away from the screen.

Explore the browser games

What’s in the Android app

The app includes English letters, Hindi वर्णमाला, numbers, colors, shapes and animals, plus learning activities and mini-games.
