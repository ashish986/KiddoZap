About KiddoZap

Founded by Ashish Pal • Independent Educational Gaming Initiative

KiddoZap (https://kiddozap.com/) was created with a clear and singular purpose: to provide school students and curious minds with wholesome, non-violent, intellectually stimulating browser games that respect their time, privacy, and developing attention spans.

The Founder's Story: Ashish Pal

Ashish Pal is an independent software engineer and educational technology advocate based in New Delhi, India. In mid-2025, while observing his young relatives play casual games on mobile web portals, he was alarmed by the relentless influx of deceptive "You Won an iPhone!" popups, unmoderated predatory chat boxes, and slot-machine style loot boxes.

Believing that the open web could do infinitely better, Ashish set out to build KiddoZap from the ground up: an uncompromising, clean-engineered sanctuary containing exactly 20 hand-crafted educational games with zero barriers to entry.

Our Three Ironclad Commitments

1. Absolute Walled Safety: Zero Chat, Zero Profiles

We do not operate chat rooms, user profiles, or messaging channels. Children playing on KiddoZap can never be contacted, messaged, or solicited by digital strangers.

2. Zero Predatory Monetization

No KiddoCoins, no VIP Passes, and no locked levels. Every game is 100% playable for free from start to finish. We respect family finances.

3. Client-Side High Scores & Privacy

High scores and game achievements are saved strictly within your browser's local cache. We do not harvest student names, school IDs, or location coordinates.
