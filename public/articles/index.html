Learning ideas for families

Three ways to bring the skills from KiddoZap’s games and music into ordinary play.

A tiny first-sound hunt

Pick a sound your child knows and find two objects that begin with it. Try a sock and a spoon for S. Say each word slowly. Let your child add a third object or draw one if it is hard to find.

Next, try a word with a different first sound and ask your child to sort it into its own group. Try a round of ABC Garden together when they are ready.

Same count, different arrangement

Start with four cups in a row. Ask your child to touch each cup as they count. Move the cups into a circle and ask whether there are still four. Count again together to check.

On another day, let your child move the cups while you guess the total. Counting Basket uses the same one-touch-per-object idea.

Answer a tune with a clap

Listen to one line of a rhyme. Pause the recording and clap its rhythm together, then replay the line to compare. On Rainbow Piano, play C twice and let your child reply with any two notes.
