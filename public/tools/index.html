Free Learning Tools

Besides games, KiddoZap has a few simple tools for practising skills at home or in class. They are free, need no sign-up, and nothing you type is stored or sent anywhere.

Times Table Practice

Choose a times table, answer 10 or 20 questions and see which facts to practise again. Good for ages 7 to 11.

Multiplication Chart

A clean 1 to 12 chart you can print. Tap a square to highlight its row and column, and look for number patterns.
