About KiddoZap

🎮 Free games for kids and teens

KiddoZap is a small, independent website where kids and teens can play simple browser games and puzzles for free. The games run in the browser, so there is nothing to install and no account to create.

Why KiddoZap exists

KiddoZap was built so that children have a place to play games and pick up useful skills along the way. Math games help with quick calculation, puzzles like Sudoku and Four in a Line help with planning and patience, and fast arcade games help with timing and hand-eye coordination. The goal is simple: screen time that is fun and also a little useful.

🧩 Play and learn

Math, memory, word and logic games sit next to arcade games, so children can practise different skills.

🚪 No sign-up

There are no accounts, passwords or chat features. Scores and stars are saved in your own browser on your device.

💻 Works on phones and computers

Games run in a normal web browser on phones, tablets and computers.

How we choose games

Every game on KiddoZap is meant to be easy to start, suitable for children, and free of graphic violence. We try to keep the games simple to understand, and we remove games that are broken or not a good fit for kids. Each game guide explains how to play, what controls to use and what skills the game practises.

Ads and privacy

KiddoZap is free to play and is supported by advertising. Google AdSense and its partners may use cookies on this site. We do not ask players for their name, email address or location. You can read the details, including how to manage ad personalisation, in our Privacy Policy.

Who runs KiddoZap

KiddoZap is an independent project run by one person. If you find a bug, have an idea for a game, or have a question or complaint, please get in touch. Contact details are on the Contact page.
