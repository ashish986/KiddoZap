Frequently asked questions

  • Can KiddoZap be used at school?
    Every school network is set up differently. If a game does not open on a school device, please ask your school's IT team whether the site can be allowed.
  • Can I request a custom math or vocabulary game?
    You can send your idea to support@kiddozap.com with the subject line "Game request". We cannot promise to build every request, but we read them all.
