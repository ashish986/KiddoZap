KiddoZap Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Welcome to KiddoZap (https://kiddozap.com/). This page explains what information is and is not collected when you use the site, how ads work, and how to contact us. KiddoZap is a free games website that children, teens and families may enjoy, so we have tried to keep this policy short and clear.

In short:
  • There are no accounts, passwords or chat features on KiddoZap, and we do not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers or locations.
  • Scores, stars, badges and settings are saved in your own browser on your device (localStorage).
  • The site is supported by advertising. Google AdSense and its partners may use cookies. See section 3.

1. Children

KiddoZap does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. There are no profiles, chat rooms or messaging features through which a child could post personal details. We encourage parents and guardians to supervise young children online. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, please write to support@kiddozap.com and we will delete it.

2. Information stored on your device

To save your progress, KiddoZap uses your browser's local storage to keep:

  • Game high scores and completed puzzle milestones.
  • Earned stars and trophies.
  • Sound and language preferences and the chosen theme.

This data stays on your device and is not sent to our servers. Clearing your browser data erases it.

3. Advertising and cookies

KiddoZap uses third-party advertising partners, including Google AdSense, to pay for running the site.

  • Google AdSense and cookies: Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on KiddoZap. These cookies can allow Google and its partners to show ads based on visits to KiddoZap and other sites.
  • DoubleClick cookie: Google uses the DoubleClick cookie in ads on publisher sites. You can manage personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings.
  • Third-party vendors: Ad networks may use cookies, JavaScript or web beacons and automatically receive your IP address when ads load. KiddoZap has no access to or control over these technologies.
  • Opting out: You can opt out of personalised advertising from participating networks at www.aboutads.info/choices or YourAdChoices. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
  • Learn more: How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Parents may wish to review the ad settings on their child's device or account and to talk to children about not clicking on ads they do not understand.

4. Log files

Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information when pages are requested, such as IP address, browser type, date and time, and the pages visited. We use this only to keep the site running, understand general traffic and prevent abuse.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom), you may have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted. KiddoZap does not require registration and does not keep personal profiles, so there is usually very little data to access. To make a request, write to us using the address below.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@kiddozap.com. More details are on our Contact page.
